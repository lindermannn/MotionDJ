# MotionDJ user guide

**English** · [Español](GUIA.md)

Two levels: the **EASY view** if you have never mixed, and the **DJ view** if you already know how. The interface is in Spanish, so the on-screen labels are shown in **bold** with their meaning. The same guide is inside the app under **? Ayuda → Guía de uso**.

## Before you start (everyone)

1. Open the app in **Chrome or Edge**.
2. Click **? Ayuda → Inicio guiado** (guided start). It walks you through:
   1. **Activar audio** (enable audio).
   2. **Cargar demos** (load demo tracks), or **Importar música** / **Elegir carpeta…** (import music / choose a folder) to use your own songs.
   3. **Activar cámara** (enable camera). Stand 1–1.5 m away, hands visible, with light in front of you.
3. No camera? Everything works with **mouse, keyboard or touch**.
4. Want to see it in action first? **? Ayuda → Demo guiada** (guided demo).

> If the stems server (Meta Demucs) is running, every song you load is split automatically into **VOZ, BATERÍA, BAJO, RESTO** (vocals, drums, bass, other). The deck shows "STEMS 45 %" and then "STEMS ✓".

## 🟢 Never mixed before: EASY view

Click **FÁCIL** (easy) at the top. The screen simplifies and the jargon goes away.

### Your first mix in 4 steps

1. Pick a song, click **LOAD A**, then **PLAY** on deck A. It's playing.
2. Pick another song and click **LOAD B**, without pressing PLAY.
3. Click **Igualar y mezclar** (match and mix): the app matches both tempos and moves from A to B smoothly in 8 seconds.
4. Want to do it yourself? Move the **crossfader** (the bar in the middle) from A to B. With the camera: make a **pinch** (thumb and index together) over the crossfader and drag.

**Let it mix by itself:** turn on **AUTO DJ** in the mixer and it goes A → B → A.

### Have fun with your hands

1. Click **MANOS · FX** (hands · FX) on the deck that is playing. The console steps aside.
2. Pick an effect with a **pinch** on its card.
3. Try:
   - ✋ **FRENO** (brake): hold a still palm, like saying "stop". The song brakes like vinyl; remove your hand and it comes back.
   - ☝️ **ROLL**: raise 1, 2 or 3 fingers. It repeats the beat; more fingers, faster. Close your hand and the song continues.
   - 🙌 **SUBIDA** (build-up): open both hands and raise them, the tension grows. 🙏 Press your palms together to release the **DROP**.
4. To leave, **take both hands out** of the camera view.

> Don't worry about timing: the app waits for the beat or the bar, so it always sounds on time.

**Record your mix:** **● Grabar mezcla** (record mix), then **■ Detener y descargar** (stop and download).

## 🔵 Already a DJ: DJ view

Click **DJ** at the top for the full console.

### Decks

- **PLAY / CUE / SYNC**: SYNC matches the BPM and aligns the phase to the beat grid.
- **Tempo ±8 %** with a center detent.
- **Zoomed waveform** of 4, 8 or 16 s to see the kicks on the grid.
- **Jog wheel**: the center scrubs **with audio forward and backward**; the edge nudges the tempo and springs back.
- **Fixed pads**: 2 hot cues, 2 four-beat jumps, 2 effects and 2 sounds. **LOOP 4**.

### Mixer

- **EQ** HIGH/MID/LOW from −26 to +6 dB, **TRIM**.
- One-knob **FILTRO** (filter): one side cuts highs, the other cuts lows.
- BPM-synced **ECO** (echo) per channel.
- **Faders, crossfader and MASTER** with a limiter.
- Warnings for **"dos graves"** (two bass lines at once) and **SATURACIÓN** (clipping).

### Lineup

- In the library, tag each track **+A** or **+B** to build each deck's queue.
- When a deck becomes free, its next track loads by itself; **⏭ SIGUE** (next) loads it now.
- **PISTAS** (tracks) opens a full-screen picker to choose with your fingers while the other deck plays.

### Headphone cue

In **⚙ Ajustes → Elegir auriculares…** (settings → choose headphones) pick your headphones output. The mix keeps playing on the main output while you pre-listen to the other deck. The cue is not included in the recording.

### Advanced hand mode

| Effect | Gesture | Fine control |
|---|---|---|
| **FILTRO** (filter) | Two pinches, stretch like a string | Choose the knob: **FILTRO, HIGH, MID, LOW, TRIM or ECO**. Apart = up, together = down |
| **ECO** (echo) | ✊ Fist ~0.3 s to grab, raise = more echo, 👍 to throw | The other deck enters **on the same beat, tempo matched**. A second fist controls the tail. A → B → A without leaving |
| **ROLL** | 1, 2 or 3 fingers = 1, ½ or ¼ beat | With stems, choose the part (e.g. only **BATERÍA**, drums). The track keeps running underneath (*slip*) |
| **FRENO** (brake) | ✋ Still palm ~0.4 s | With stems, brake one part or **MÚSICA** (everything but the vocals). Returns on the beat, in place |
| **SUBIDA → DROP** (build-up) | 🙌 Both hands up, 🙏 palms together | Drum roll gets shorter, bass drops out, high-pass opens. The DROP lands on the next bar |
| **STEMS** | 🤏 Pinch VOZ, BATERÍA, BAJO or RESTO | Mutes/unmutes that part on the beat; two pinches = its volume |

### Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| `Q` / `A` | Deck A |
| `P` / `L` | Deck B |
| `←` `→` | Crossfader |
| `G` | Pause gestures without stopping the music |
| `F` | Fullscreen |
| `Esc` | Leave hand mode |

**Camera:** under **📷 Cámara** choose **ESPEJO** (mirror) and **Encuadre COMPLETO / RECORTE** (full / crop framing). In **⚙ Ajustes** you can tune how long the app waits before leaving hand mode when your hands go out of view.

## Tips for reliable gestures

- **Light in front of you**, not behind.
- **Clear gestures, held for a moment**: the app waits a few milliseconds so it doesn't get confused.
- **Inside the frame**, at chest height.
- If something odd happens, `G` pauses gestures instantly.
